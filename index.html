About

From Hilbert spaces to production data.

I spent five years at the University of Gdańsk doing research in quantum information theory, building Python and MATLAB code to verify mathematical conjectures and solving semidefinite programs on problems most people never see. That taught me to break huge, ambiguous problems into testable pieces — and to be suspicious of any number I can't reproduce.

Today I work on the applied side: data pipelines, dashboards, cloud services and automation. I help run science-popularisation projects at the Centrum Nauki Experyment in Gdynia, and I build software for environmental and IoT monitoring at Embedded Systems. I learn new tools fast, explain technical things plainly, and enjoy working with both engineers and non-technical stakeholders.

Featured · Open source

BENTOS: a smart buoy for the Gulf of Gdańsk

Every summer, the Gulf of Gdańsk raises the same questions: Is it safe to swim today? Is there a bloom? BENTOS is a joint effort of Embedded Systems and the Institute of Oceanology of the Polish Academy of Sciences (IO PAN), co-financed by the EU programme Fundusze Europejskie dla Pomorza, to answer them with data.

An autonomous measuring buoy collects in-situ water-quality readings. They are combined with satellite and model products and fed into a predictive model developed with IO PAN. The result is a public web and mobile app that shows current water quality and a forecast of risks for local residents. The whole system is designed to be open source.

My part: the software side — data pipelines, APIs and the public-facing dashboards. I also presented the project to the public at the Researchers' Night (Noc Naukowców) and I am writing a popular-science article on the physics behind the signals.
