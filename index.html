About

From Hilbert spaces to production data.

I spent five years in quantum-information research at the University of Gdańsk, learning to turn abstract questions into results that anyone can reproduce. Along the way I wrote tools in Python and MATLAB, solved a lot of semidefinite programs and presented my work in Seoul and Kyoto.

Today I lead the software team at EmbeddedSystems.do, where we build environmental and IoT monitoring products: data pipelines, dashboards, cloud services and automation. I like making complex systems genuinely useful, and I always leave a little room for curiosity.

◎ Gulf of Gdańsk · Baltic Sea

A sea of data.
A clearer picture.

What's happening beneath the surface? We're building the tools to find out.

BENTOS brings together in-situ water-quality sensors, satellite observations and predictive modelling to help people understand the Gulf of Gdańsk, from everyday conditions to risks such as cyanobacteria blooms.

Developed by EmbeddedSystems.do with the Institute of Oceanology, Polish Academy of Sciences. Designed around open data, open code and open hardware.

SatBałtyk dashboard

A Python pipeline turns gigabyte-scale GeoTIFF exports into lightweight map layers. A React app makes them explorable: time playback, layer comparison and point charts.
